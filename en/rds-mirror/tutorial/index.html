Flow 01 — Right after launch

Get the big picture from the Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see on launch. It shows registered RDS, running jobs, how many resources are mirrored, and whether proxy nodes are alive — all at a glance. We're starting from a clean state with nothing registered yet.

1
①The top cards show counts; the bottom section shows per-RDS reachability and the job list. Next, head to Mirror on the left.

Flow 02 — RDS Registry

Register the source and destination RDS

Go to RDS Registry in the left sidebar and register the two registries you'll mirror between. We start with STUDIO-A already registered, then add STUDIO-B via + Add registry in the top-right. Just fill in the Query API URL and Registration API URL and save.

Flow 03 — Mirror workspace

Mirror a resource as-is (Passthrough)

Go to Mirror in the left sidebar, pick STUDIO-A as SOURCE and STUDIO-B as DESTINATION. Click a sender/receiver in the left pane to select it, then press Copy selection in the middle — it's registered on the right side with the same UUID and structure.

①Pick STUDIO-A in the SOURCE dropdown — resources are listed by node and device.
2
②Pick STUDIO-B in the DESTINATION dropdown. The right side is still empty at this point.

4
③④Click the row you want to mirror (CAM-1 Video), then press Copy selection.

⑤It's registered on the right, with a connection line drawn between them. Click the icon on the line to move on to Transform.
Passthrough mode copies a sender/receiver's UUID and structure as-is. If you need to rewrite the IP address or label on the destination side, use Transform mode instead — covered next.

Flow 04 — Transform config

Rewrite the destination IP/port while mirroring (Transform)

Click the icon at the center of the connection line to open the config panel. Switch to the Transform tab, enter the destination IP/port under Destination (G), and use Preview SDP / active JSON to check the exact SDP and active JSON that will be published — before you save. Saving registers the resource on the destination RDS through a dedicated proxy node.

Flow 05 — Resource detail

Double-click a resource to check its provenance

Double-click any sender/receiver row to open its detail modal. A badge tells you whether it's natively registered here or mirrored in from another RDS (which job, and what its original ID was), and you can inspect the raw JSON as well.

①ORIGINAL — a resource natively registered on this RDS (opened by double-clicking its row).
②TRANSFORM (PROXY) — shows which job, and which RDS, it was mirrored from.
6

Flow 06 — Chain-mirror guard / Jobs

Prevent chain mirroring, and watch job status in Jobs

What happens if you try to mirror a resource that was itself mirrored in, onward to yet another RDS? By default, chain mirroring is blocked — "mirrored-in" and "chain blocked" markers appear at the row, device, and node level, and trying to select one shows a toast explaining why (toggle it on via "Allow chain mirroring" in Settings). The Jobs page gives you a rundown of every running job's status — enabled/disabled, heartbeat, and resource count.

1
①A resource that was mirrored in gets "mirrored-in" and "chain blocked" markers. Try clicking it.
②Clicking it shows a toast explaining why it's blocked and how to change the setting.
③The Jobs page rounds up every running job's enabled/disabled state and heartbeat status.
※ Logs and Settings aren't covered in detail here — see the manual guide or README if you need them
