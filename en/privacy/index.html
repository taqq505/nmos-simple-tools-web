Privacy Policy

Cookies and analytics on this site

Last updated: August 31, 2026

NMOS Simple Tools ("this site") is a website showcasing open-source tools for broadcast engineers. This page explains how cookies and analytics are used here.

Use of Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) only, to understand how the site is used and to improve its content.

Analytics cookies are only enabled if you choose "Accept" on the banner shown on your first visit. If you choose "Decline," no analytics cookies are stored and no data that could identify you is sent.

About Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4), an analytics service provided by Google. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized usage statistics — pages viewed, time on site, referral source, and approximate location. This data does not include information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

Data collected is handled according to Google's own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking entirely by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising (Planned)

We plan to show third-party ads (e.g. Google AdSense) on this site in the future. If and when ads are introduced, the ad provider may use cookies for ad personalization. As with analytics cookies, we will request consent beforehand and update this policy accordingly.

Access Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. General server access logs (such as IP address and access time) may be processed by our hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice to reflect changes in law or in how this site operates. Any update takes effect as soon as it is posted on this page.
